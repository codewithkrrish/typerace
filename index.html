    How the numbers work

    • WPM = (correct characters ÷ 5) ÷ minutes. A "word" is 5 characters, the standard typing-test definition — so only characters you typed correctly count towards your WPM.
    • Raw WPM counts every character you typed, right or wrong. The gap between raw and real WPM is your typo rate.
    • Accuracy is correct characters ÷ all characters typed. Consistency measures how steady your speed was second by second — a flat pace scores near 100%.
    • The test ends when the timer runs out or you finish the whole text, whichever comes first. Short texts in long modes just end early.
    • Your best scores never leave this browser. There is no account, no sync, and no leaderboard.
